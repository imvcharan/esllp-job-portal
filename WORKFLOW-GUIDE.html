esllp Portal Feature & Workflow Guide

A practical, step-by-step reference for using the live job portal: create and secure accounts, find and apply for roles, run an employer hiring pipeline, and administer the platform.

How to use this guide

This guide describes user-visible workflows currently implemented in the repository. Use the contents links to jump by role, or search topics above. A capability described as available may still depend on deployment configuration—for example, email delivery, private file storage, or Razorpay checkout. Where an action is role-restricted, the guide calls that out. Candidate deletion requests are reviewed but do not trigger automatic deletion. Labels may vary slightly as the interface evolves.

01

Roles & access at a glance

Access is enforced by the server as well as the interface.

Account Primary workspace Typical access
Candidate Candidate workspace Manage own profile and documents, search and save jobs, submit applications, follow application status, and manage notifications.
Employer Employer workspace Manage company-owned listings and applicants, coordinate interviews and team access, review notifications, and manage plan settings according to company role.
Staff Admin workspace Manage job listings and review platform-level hiring metrics, companies, and candidate-stage aggregates. Candidate account details are reserved for superadmins.
Admin Admin workspace Staff workspace plus recent platform activity.
Superadmin Admin workspace Admin capabilities plus user-account administration and the protected audit log.
Company roles are separate from platform roles. Employer organization membership can additionally be Owner, Admin, Recruiter, Hiring manager, or Billing. Company roles scope employer actions inside an organization; they do not grant access to the platform admin dashboard.
02

Accounts, verification & sign-in

Shared registration, authentication, recovery, and session workflows.

Create a candidate account

  1. From the public homepage, choose Create account, then select the candidate account option.
  2. Enter your name, email address, and password. The password must meet the form's minimum length requirement.
  3. Submit registration. The portal sends an email verification link; open it to verify the address before signing in.
  4. If the message does not arrive, use Resend verification from the sign-in flow and check the address and spam folder.

Create an employer account

  1. Choose For employers or the employer account option during registration.
  2. Enter your name, work email, password, company name, and optionally the company website.
  3. Verify your email address using the link sent to your inbox.
  4. Wait for an administrator to approve the employer account. Until approval, employer access is limited to a read-only overview, company profile, and existing job listings.
Invited teammate? Open the company's invitation link and register with the exact email address to which the invitation was sent. Invitations expire after 30 days. Email verification and employer approval are still required before employer actions unlock.

Sign in, recover, and sign out

  • Use Log in with the account email and password. The portal sends each role to its own workspace.
  • Use Forgot password? to request a reset email, then follow the link and choose a replacement password.
  • Use Resend verification if an account still needs email verification.
  • Use the account's Change password form to change a known password. Existing refresh sessions are revoked, so sign in again afterward.
  • Use Log out when finished, especially on shared devices.
First admin account: When no superadmin exists and the deployment has enabled one-time setup, the configured superadmin can be created at /setup-superadmin/. Setup is closed after the first superadmin is created.
03

Candidate workflows

Build a profile, find relevant roles, apply with explicit sharing choices, and follow progress.

3.1 Build and manage your profile

  1. Sign in as a candidate and open My profile. The profile strength indicator reflects completion of key sections.
  2. Add or update your name, professional headline, preferred location, optional phone, summary, and skills. Skills are entered as comma-separated values.
  3. Add work experience and education entries. You can add, edit, or remove entries; the editor supports multiple entries in each section.
  4. Set target job titles, preferred employment types, and workplace arrangements (on-site, hybrid, or remote).
  5. Add professional links such as LinkedIn, portfolio/website, and GitHub.
  6. Choose Save profile and wait for the confirmation that the server saved your changes.

Profile photo

Upload a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image up to 6 MB. Reposition and zoom in the crop tool, save the crop, or remove the photo later. The stored image is private.

Resume

Upload a valid PDF up to 8 MB, replace it, or remove it. The resume is private and is not attached to applications automatically.

Profile privacy is opt-in. Your profile remains private unless you enable profile sharing in privacy preferences and separately choose to share it when submitting an application. The employer receives a snapshot for that application. Resume attachment is a separate per-application choice.

Profile and email preferences

  • Allow sharing my profile details when I apply: permits the profile-sharing option in an application; it does not share your profile by itself.
  • Email me application status and interview updates: controls those email updates. In-app notifications remain available when email is turned off.
  • Email me about matching new jobs: enables job-alert emails based on target role titles and selected work preferences when a matching job is published.
  • Candidate account email is displayed read-only in the profile editor.

3.2 Discover, search, and save jobs

  1. Open Find jobs in the candidate workspace or search from the public homepage.
  2. Search by role, skill, or company, and optionally by location. Use available filters to narrow by work arrangement, employment type, experience, company, salary and pay period, or listing freshness.
  3. Change sort order where available and use pagination to browse additional results.
  4. Open a listing to review company, location, compensation (when supplied), experience, deadline, openings, and job description.
  5. Select the save control on a published job to add it to Saved jobs. Use the same control again to remove it.
  6. Open Saved jobs to revisit a saved role from your account on another device. Remove a saved role if it is no longer relevant or has closed.
Guest saves: saves made before sign-in may exist only in that browser. Sign in to sync eligible published-job saves to your account. Preview/sample roles are not live vacancies and cannot be applied to.

Save a search and enable alerts

  1. Run a job search and select the desired query, location, and filters.
  2. Choose Save this search, enter a name, and save it to your account. Up to 25 searches can be saved.
  3. Open Saved searches to run a saved search again, update its alert preference, or delete it.
  4. Enable Instant email alerts on a saved search to receive an email when a newly published job matches its saved criteria. Alerts depend on email delivery being configured for the deployment.

3.3 Submit an application

  1. Open a live published job from search results or a saved-job list. Sample/preview roles are informational only.
  2. Review the job details and confirm it is still open and within its application deadline.
  3. Optionally enter a short note or cover letter.
  4. Choose whether to attach the resume currently stored in your profile. This choice is optional and must be made for each application.
  5. Your account name is always shared with the employer. If profile sharing is enabled in your preferences, choose separately whether to share your additional profile snapshot and contact details.
  6. Submit once. The application starts at Submitted and appears under Applications.
One active application per job: duplicate active applications are prevented. If the employer confirms your withdrawal and the job remains open, you may apply again; your earlier application history remains in your account.

Track, update, or withdraw applications

  • Open Applications to see the job, company, location, submission date, current status, and status history.
  • Statuses may include Submitted In review Interview Offer Offer expired Rejected Withdrawn.
  • Where the employer adds a candidate-facing note, it appears in the status timeline.
  • Request withdrawal at any active stage. The application remains in a pending-withdrawal state until the employer confirms or declines your request. If confirmed, you may reapply while the job remains open.
  • Use the application message thread to contact the hiring team. Messages are text-only and visible to the candidate and authorized members of the employer's company team.
  • When you receive an offer, review the employer-entered summary and deadline, then accept or decline before it expires. The summary is not a generated contract. Expired offers cannot be accepted or declined and appear as Offer expired.
  • Open Notifications to review application status, messages, offers, and interview updates, open related pages, and mark notifications as read. In-app updates remain available when application email updates are turned off.

Candidate account security

Change your password from the candidate profile's Account security section. After a successful change, sign in again. Password-reset and email-verification links are sent by email and must be completed before their intended action can finish.

Export your data or request account deletion

  • Open My profile and choose Download my data to save a JSON export of your account details, profile, application history, saved jobs/searches, notifications, offer details and responses, and application messages. Private storage object paths and file contents are not included.
  • Use Request account deletion review to submit a request to the Superadmin queue. The request is reviewed manually; submitting it does not disable sign-in or delete account data, applications, or files.
  • Deletion and retention rules still require an approved privacy/legal policy. Contact support if you need an update about a request.
04

Employer workflows

Set up an approved company workspace, publish vacancies, collaborate on candidates, and coordinate the team.

4.1 Register and get approved

Register employer→Verify email→Administrator review→Approved hiring access
  • Register with your name, work email, password, company name, and optional website.
  • Verify the email address. Administrators cannot approve an employer until the address is verified.
  • While approval is pending, sign in to see a read-only overview and review the company profile and any existing job listings. Publishing, editing, applicant review, team, interviews, and billing are unavailable until approval.
  • After an administrator approves the account, sign in again if necessary. Employer tools are then available subject to your organization role and plan.
  • If rejected, contact the platform administrator or support for next steps; the rejected account cannot use employer tools.

4.2 Choose an organization

If your user belongs to more than one company organization, use the organization selector in the workspace to switch context. Company jobs, applications, team membership, and plan limits are scoped to the selected organization. Only organizations where you hold a membership are available.

4.3 Complete the company profile

  1. Open Company profile after approval.
  2. Review or update organization information, such as company name, website, industry, company size, and company description.
  3. Save the profile and check the confirmation message.

Create, edit, publish, and close a job

  1. Open Manage jobs and choose Create a job draft.
  2. Enter the required job title and location. Add workplace type, employment type, salary range, salary currency and pay period, number of openings, application deadline, experience, and description as appropriate.
  3. Choose Save draft. A draft remains private to the company and does not consume a published-job slot.
  4. Use Edit to revise a listing. Review the full listing before publishing.
  5. Add a job description of at least 100 characters before publishing. Salary disclosure is optional; clearly describe the role, location/work arrangement, and requirements. Choose Publish to make it discoverable to candidates, subject to plan capacity and approval.
  6. Choose Close when the role is no longer accepting applications. Closed roles are no longer open listings.

The job list shows role, location, salary, openings, deadline, application count, published/draft status, and creation date. Use pagination when the company has more listings than fit on one page.

Plan limits: The workspace displays active-job and seat limits. Drafts do not use a published job slot. If capacity is reached, review Plan & billing or ask the company owner/billing manager.

4.4 Review and manage applicants

  1. Open Applicants. Search by candidate or role, then filter by pipeline stage and job listing.
  2. Review the application stage, job, submission date, optional candidate note, and application history.
  3. Review profile details only when the candidate chose to share their profile with that application. If profile sharing was not selected, the application says the details were not shared.
  4. Open a temporary private resume link only when the candidate chose to attach a resume to that application. No resume is attached by default.
  5. Move an eligible application to In review or Rejected. Scheduling an interview automatically moves a Submitted or In review application to Interview; the candidate receives an in-app update. Add an optional status note when appropriate.
  6. Use Send offer to enter an offer summary and response deadline. The candidate can accept or decline; this feature does not produce a contract or determine legal effect.
  7. Resolve a pending candidate withdrawal request by confirming or declining it. Declining restores the previous pipeline stage; confirming permits the candidate to apply again while the job remains open.
  8. Use the application message thread for text-only communication with the candidate. Company-authorized hiring members can see the thread.
  9. Select eligible applicants and use the bulk status form to update several records on the current page. Optionally add a candidate-facing note.
  10. Add internal team notes for company-only context. These are distinct from candidate-facing status notes.
  11. Add tags to help organize applicants, and remove a tag when it is no longer useful.
Candidate privacy: Review only applications for jobs owned by your organization. Profile snapshots and attached resumes are exposed only according to the candidate's application choices. Internal notes are for your company team; do not put sensitive or unrelated information there.

Application stage transitions

Current stage Next stages available to employer Notes
Submitted In review, Interview, Offer, Rejected Scheduling an interview moves the stage automatically. Offer requires an offer summary and deadline.
In review Interview, Offer, Rejected Changes are added to the application history.
Interview Offer, Rejected Scheduling and stage are synchronized.
Offer Accepted, Declined The candidate responds to the offer summary before the deadline.
Rejected, Withdrawn, Offer accepted/declined No further employer transition A withdrawal request must be confirmed or declined by the employer.

Schedule and manage interviews

  • From an applicant's record, choose a start date/time, duration, and optionally a meeting URL to schedule an interview.
  • Open Interviews to review upcoming interview records.
  • Cancel an interview if plans change. Interview updates can appear in the candidate's notifications and hiring updates.
  • Check the candidate's notification area for interview invitations and application updates.

4.5 Invite teammates and manage access

  1. Open Team & access. Owner and Admin members can invite a teammate by work email and choose a company role.
  2. Choose the role that matches the work: Recruiter (manage jobs and candidates), Hiring manager (review candidates), Billing (manage subscription), or Admin (manage company and team).
  3. Send the invitation. The invited person follows the email link and registers using the invited email address. Links expire after 30 days; an owner/admin can resend an invitation.
  4. After the teammate accepts, verifies email, and receives required employer approval, their organization access reflects the assigned company role.
  5. Use the member controls to adjust an existing member's company access when authorized.

4.6 Plan, billing & employer notifications

  • Open Plan & billing to see the current plan, subscription status, period end, active-job usage, seat allowance, and available plans.
  • Eligible owners or members with billing permission can choose a paid plan when checkout is configured. Complete the Razorpay checkout and return to the workspace to verify the subscription.
  • Only the company owner or a member with billing access can change subscription settings. If the action is disabled, ask an authorized member.
  • Use Cancel at renewal to stop a paid subscription at its renewal boundary when available. If a subscription is halted/cancelled, the organization returns to Free limits; excess active jobs may be closed and are not automatically republished after an upgrade.
  • Open Notifications for new application, interview, and company updates. Mark an unread item as read.
Deployment dependency: Paid checkout is available only when Razorpay keys, plan IDs, configured prices, and a verified webhook are set up on the server. Do not enter payment credentials into the portal or share them with teammates.
05

Admin workflows

Operational and moderation workflows in the protected admin dashboard.

Sign in and understand role scope

  1. Open /admin/ and sign in with an active Staff, Admin, or Superadmin account.
  2. Use the sidebar to open Overview, Job listings, Reports, Candidates, Companies, Analytics, and Account security.
  3. Admins and Superadmins also have Recent activity. Superadmins additionally have User accounts, Privacy requests, and Audit log.
  4. Use Refresh on pages that provide it to fetch current records. Use the page search field to filter the visible table/list.
Least privilege: Staff/Admin cannot open superadmin-only user administration or audit routes. Candidate account details are restricted to Superadmins; staff/admin users see only aggregate candidate and application-stage metrics in that area.

5.1 Review and manage job listings

  • Overview: review active job, account, application, and scheduled-interview counts; application-stage summary; top listings; and available recent activity. Use Create a job to add a listing.
  • Job listings: search by title/company/location and filter by active or draft state and company.
  • Create or edit a listing with its role, company, location, workplace/employment type, salary details, openings, deadline, experience, description, and employer assignment as applicable.
  • Change listing fields with the edit action. Archive a listing to remove it from active administration/search results. Use CSV export for the job list.
  • Active jobs are candidate-visible; drafts are not. Verify status and listing content after saving.

5.2 Review candidate pipeline and companies

  • Candidates: view application-stage totals (including interview and offer counts). Superadmins can search, paginate, and export candidate account details, including account status and email verification.
  • Companies: review registered organization, industry, plan, open-job count, team size, and join date. Refresh to retrieve the latest list.
  • Candidate page stage totals are aggregates, not a substitute for opening an employer's company-scoped applicant pipeline.

5.3 Review analytics and activity

  • Analytics: review total applications, scheduled interviews, candidate accounts, and employer organizations; compare weekly application counts and current application stages.
  • Source attribution, hires, and time-to-hire are not tracked, so they are intentionally not reported.
  • Recent activity: Admins and Superadmins can review recent job, account, and employer workflow events. Sensitive audit detail belongs in the Superadmin Audit log.
  • When no events exist yet, the activity page shows an empty-state message rather than an activity record.

5.4 Manage accounts (Superadmin)

  1. Open User accounts. Search by name/email, filter by role and active status, and use pagination to locate an account.
  2. Review company, role, employer approval, email verification, password setup, account status, and join date.
  3. To create a managed Staff, Admin, or Employer account, enter name, email, role, and a temporary password. Employer accounts also require a company name; website is optional.
  4. Give the temporary password to the intended user through an approved secure channel. On first sign-in, the user must replace it before proceeding.
  5. For an employer created by Superadmin, the account starts pending approval and a company organization is created. Approve it only after verifying the email and reviewing the request.
  6. Use role controls to assign an allowed platform role, or activate/deactivate an account as appropriate. Deactivation revokes refresh sessions.
Guardrails: An employer cannot be created by changing an existing account's role; use employer registration or the managed employer-account form, then review employer approval separately. The last active Superadmin cannot be demoted or deactivated, and an administrator cannot deactivate their own account.

5.5 Review reports and privacy requests

  • Reports: staff, admins, and superadmins can filter the queue by status, review job/company/user report metadata, start review, record an action, or dismiss a report. Add an optional resolution note before changing status.
  • Report statuses follow Open → Reviewing → Actioned or Dismissed. A completed report cannot be edited. Report creation and status changes are recorded in the audit log.
  • The public job detail page currently lets signed-in candidates and employers report a job listing. Company and account targets are supported by the authenticated API but do not yet have public reporting controls.
  • Privacy requests: only Superadmins can review candidate deletion requests. They can start review or defer a request with an explanation. This queue does not delete accounts or files; approved deletion, anonymization, retention, and backup rules must be defined and carried out separately.

Review and decide an employer approval

  1. Locate the employer in User accounts and inspect company identity, email verification, and account status.
  2. Approve only after the employer has verified their email. Approval enables company actions subject to membership permissions and plan limits.
  3. Reject if the request should not proceed. Rejection revokes active refresh sessions and employer access.
  4. Record any required operational follow-up through the established support process. Approval/rejection is recorded in the audit history.

5.6 Inspect the audit log (Superadmin)

  • Open Audit log to search records by actor, action, target, IP/client details, or text.
  • Optionally filter by exact action, country, and date range. Apply filters, clear them, refresh, and page through matching records.
  • Export matching records as CSV when needed for an authorized review.
  • Audit entries are immutable through the interface: they cannot be edited or deleted.
  • Approximate location is based on IP lookup using a locally configured GeoIP database, when present. It is not browser GPS, and the application does not send IPs to a third-party location lookup for this display.

Admin password security

Open Account security, enter the current password, a new password, and its confirmation. After a successful change, the dashboard signs you out; sign in with the new password.

Common questions & troubleshooting

What to check when a workflow is blocked or a page is empty.

“I can't sign in.”

Confirm the email address and password, complete email verification if requested, and use password recovery if needed. A deactivated account or expired session may require administrator assistance.

“My employer tools are read-only.”

Check the workspace status. Pending employers can view overview/company/jobs only. Complete email verification and wait for Superadmin approval.

“I can't attach a resume.”

Upload a valid PDF to your candidate profile first. It must be no larger than 8 MB. Then explicitly select it on the application form.

“The employer cannot see my profile.”

Profile visibility requires both enabling profile sharing in preferences and choosing to share on that application. It is never automatic.

“I don't see a job I saved.”

Closed/unpublished listings may no longer be available. Guest saves and preview listings may be local to the browser; sign in to sync eligible published jobs.

“I can't publish a job or invite a teammate.”

Confirm employer approval, selected organization, your company role, and plan job/seat limits. Ask the organization Owner/Admin for access changes.

“Paid plan checkout is unavailable.”

The deployment must configure Razorpay credentials, plan IDs/prices, and the webhook. Contact the portal operator; never put payment secrets in browser code.

“No activity has been recorded yet.”

This means there are no recent platform activity events available to show. It is an empty state, not an error. Refresh later after platform workflows have generated events.

For application failures, note the job title, approximate time, and the message shown. For account access or company approval, contact the portal administrator/support using the support link provided in the app.

Feature boundaries & deployment notes

Distinguish live behavior from configuration-dependent behavior and metrics the product does not claim.

  • Candidate resume and photo files are private; uploads require the server's private storage integration.
  • Verification, password recovery, status updates, and matching job alerts require functioning email delivery.
  • Employer subscriptions require server-side Razorpay configuration and verified webhook processing.
  • Pending employer accounts have read-only access; employer approval is not automatic.
  • Candidate profile and resume sharing are opt-in and tied to each application.
  • Job search results contain live published listings; sample/preview content is not a live opening.
  • Administrative analytics show live counts and stages, not source attribution, completed hires, or time-to-hire.
  • The protected audit log is for Superadmins and is not editable or deletable from the portal.
  • Platform administrator roles and company membership roles are different access layers.
  • Some features may be hidden or unavailable when the signed-in role lacks permission.
For operators: This is an end-user workflow guide, not a deployment runbook. Keep credentials, environment values, private storage secrets, and payment keys out of this document and out of frontend code.
